ThermoPipe Parameter Suite

A standalone offline web application for exploring copper heat-pipe and gravity-thermosyphon parameters using n-pentane or n-hexane.

OPENGITHUB

Models

The current tool covers wickless gravity thermosyphons and annular-wick heat pipes, including vapour pressure, transport limits, charge, tube-size scanning and simplified mechanical screening.

Method

It exposes fill ratio, geometry, heat load, temperature and design margin as variables instead of presenting one universal optimum.

Scope

The current implementation is a reduced-order engineering model for exploration and sensitivity work. It is not a pressure-vessel certification tool.

Design aid only. Real sealed hardware requires validated thermal, structural, materials and safety analysis.
