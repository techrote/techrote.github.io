Brachistochrone Lab

A dependency-free browser experiment comparing three gravity-driven paths between the same two points: a cycloid, a straight ramp and an adjustable custom curve.

OPENGITHUB

Controls

Horizontal span, vertical drop, gravity, initial speed, curve bow and playback speed are adjustable. Travel time and track length are shown for each path.

Model

Each racer is treated as an ideal point mass. Speed follows conservation of mechanical energy and travel times are approximated numerically along sampled path segments.

Boundary

The model omits drag, rolling resistance, rotational inertia and other losses. It is an educational idealization, not a vehicle-dynamics simulator.
