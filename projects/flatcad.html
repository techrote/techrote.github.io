flatCAD

A local-first 2.5D browser modeller for turning XY sketches into exact solids and STEP files.

OPEN WEBAPPSOURCE

Workflow

Draw rectangles, circles and other supported profiles on the fixed XY plane. Add or cut material along Z, refine parameters, review feature history, then save the native project or export STEP.

Implementation

The authoritative model is parametric; an OpenCascade WebAssembly worker calculates boundary-representation geometry. Three.js provides the viewport, not geometric authority. The hosted build is static and has no account or server-side CAD dependency.

Status and limits

Beta 1. This is deliberately 2.5D, not arbitrary-workplane CAD. Independent STEP re-import, tolerance and manufacturing qualification remain separate acceptance work. Keep source projects and validate exported models before fabrication.
