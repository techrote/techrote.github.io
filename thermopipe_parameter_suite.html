ThermoPipe Parameter Suite

Concept sizing for straight copper two-phase thermosyphons and simple wicked heat pipes using n-pentane or n-hexane. The app exposes its assumptions and scans practical tube sizes instead of claiming a universal optimum.

    Model basis, references and limitations
    
Vapour pressure: NIST Chemistry WebBook Antoine equation, log10(P/bar)=A−B/(T+C). n-Pentane: A=3.9892, B=1070.617, C=−40.454, 268.8–341.37 K. n-Hexane: A=4.00266, B=1171.53, C=−48.784, 286.18–342.69 K; a lower-temperature NIST fit is also included.

    
Latent heat: NIST Majer–Svoboda correlations. Flooding: Faghri-style gravity thermosyphon correlation using vapour density, surface tension and Bond number. Sonic limit: one-dimensional choked-vapour screening relation. Wick mode: capillary pressure 2σcosθ/rp with Darcy liquid return and estimated vapour pressure drop.

    
Reference property anchors: PubChem/CRC values for room-temperature liquid density, viscosity and surface tension. Temperature variation for those three properties is intentionally simple and should be replaced with REFPROP/CoolProp or measured data for final design.

Pressure mechanics here are thin-wall screening only. Negative gauge pressure is reported as a vacuum case and is not checked for external-pressure collapse. Joint strength, cyclic fatigue, local thinning, copper temper, brazed/soldered joint derating and code compliance are outside scope.
